School Rules and Expectations

At Lincoln Elementary School, there are important rules that students must follow.

Students must arrive on time. They have to bring their textbooks every day. If they are sick, parents need to call the school.

Students should do their homework every night. They should ask the teacher if they don't understand something.

Students must not use phones in class. They don't have to wear a uniform, but they should dress appropriately.

Parents should attend parent-teacher conferences. They need to sign report cards.
